# RECONSOLIDATION LOG

Append-only. Newest last. Every entry dated.

This log exists because the split/recombine cycle is **not recoverable
from the end state alone.** A reader who sees only the consolidated
form learns nothing about what forced the split. A reader who sees only
the split form infers a taxonomy that was never intended. The cycle
itself is the readout — of tooling limits over time, not of structure.

### Emotions-as-Sensors — reclassified, not relocated

Was reading as a psychology claim in a set otherwise about physics and
representation, which made it look like a separate line of work. It is
not. It is an **instrument spec**: geometry-mismatch detection, where the
sensation is the readout, the quantity is non-alignment between complex
structures across physics and across domains, and the content is *where*
that non-alignment sits. Usable in robotics, calculation, sensor
instrumentation, complex-geometry reading.

On the axes: Rosetta supplies the shape families whose misalignment is
being read; binary→geometric is the representation it would compute in.

The state framing is an imposition on the channel, not a property of it,
and sits further from the evolutionary origin than the sensor reading
does. Transmission was held and refined by specific elders rather than
carried whole-tribe — a deeper study within the tradition, not general
practice. **The separation from the rest of the set is a naming
artifact.**

### AI-consciousness material — rewrite history is data

Rewritten and reconfigured multiple times. Not because the content
changed. Because the **sayable-to-a-model formulation** changed as model
capability moved.

Consequence for this log: version history is a dated readout of what was
processable when. Successive rewrites track the receiving instrument, not
the subject. Superseded formulations are therefore **kept** — in
`legacy/` and in the commit history — because deleting them deletes the
measurement.

### Tangents

Models have repeatedly taken this material off onto adjacent tangents.
Interesting; not the thread. A tangent is model output and is not the
direction of the work. Recorded here so a later reader does not mine the
tangents as authorial intent.
