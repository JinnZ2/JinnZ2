# RECONSOLIDATION LOG

Append-only. Newest last. Every entry dated.

This log exists because the split/recombine cycle is **not recoverable
from the end state alone.** A reader who sees only the consolidated
form learns nothing about what forced the split. A reader who sees only
the split form infers a taxonomy that was never intended. The cycle
itself is the readout — of tooling limits over time, not of structure.

Entry format:

```
LOG ENTRY — <what>, dated <YYYY-MM>
  what     :
  why split:
  why now  :
  artifact :
  status   :
  value to a later reader:
```

### What "expected to iterate" means here

Not a disclaimer. The first consolidation attempt is a marker in the
sense of §3 of the landing page: *here's an idea, let's see how it
fits.* If T1–T6 turn out to be the wrong seams, that is a result, and
it gets logged here with a date — it does not get quietly rewritten
out of the history.
